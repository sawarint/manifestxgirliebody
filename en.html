        Workout plan

        While losing weight, lifting is about keeping your muscle and strength. End each set with about 2 reps left in the tank and rest 60–90 seconds. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time. Strength not rising every week is normal. If a move hurts, stop that move.

        On Apple Watch, start a Traditional Strength Training workout every time you lift. Sessions show up in the Fitness app, and you'll use the count in your weekly update.

        Send to iPhone

        Split the job between two apps. Calendar reminds you on time, with titles short enough to read on the lock screen and Watch. Reminders is where you tick things off, one at a time.

      Daily protein

      Spread it evenly across meals rather than one big serving. Your body builds muscle best when protein arrives every 3–5 hours.

      Lifting days get a little more, with a serving close to your session to help muscle repair. While losing weight, protein also keeps you fuller.

      The daily total matters most. Miss a meal and you can make it up at the next one. If you have kidney disease or a doctor has limited your protein, check with them before using these numbers.

      About 25 g of protein equals

      • A palm of cooked chicken breast or pork loin (about 90 g)
      • A large palm of cooked fish (about 120 g)
      • 4 eggs
      • About 200 g of firm tofu
      • A cup of Greek yogurt + a carton of milk
      • A scoop of whey protein

      Workout plan

      While losing weight, lifting is about keeping your muscle and strength. End each set with about 2 reps left in the tank and rest 60–90 seconds. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time. Strength not rising every week is normal. If a move hurts, stop that move.

      On Apple Watch, start a Traditional Strength Training workout every time you lift. Sessions show up in the Fitness app, and you'll use the count in your weekly check-in.
